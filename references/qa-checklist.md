# Release checklist

## Brief and calculations

- [ ] Monthly volume and time window use the intended definition.
- [ ] Race date, weeks available, target pace, PB dates, and current fitness evidence are consistent.
- [ ] BMI, weekly volume, HR zones, VDOT, and equivalent performances were independently recalculated.
- [ ] HR method is explicit: %MHR for beginners/limited data; %HRR for systematic runners with resting and credible maximum HR data.
- [ ] Heart-rate bands match the five prescribed MHR/HRR intensity ranges rather than generic equal-width zones.
- [ ] Facts, inferences, assumptions, and items to verify are distinguishable.

## Coaching safety

- [ ] Injury causation is hypothetical unless clinically established.
- [ ] Pain traffic lights and stop rules are visible.
- [ ] The plan includes strength, recovery, cutback, peak, taper, and fallback sessions.
- [ ] Female physiology content is symptom-led and does not infer menopause from age.
- [ ] Supplement language avoids medical or performance guarantees.

## Sponsor content

- [ ] Each product has a functional role, test session, data point, and failure signal.
- [ ] Official/current product claims and images have sources.
- [ ] No branded product was synthesized or materially altered.
- [ ] Product images use contain fitting and are fully visible.
- [ ] Sponsor content remains subordinate to coaching logic.

## Presentation quality

- [ ] Each slide has one clear conclusion-led title.
- [ ] Body text is legible at presentation distance.
- [ ] Tables fit without clipping, tiny type, or false precision.
- [ ] Images preserve aspect ratio and important content.
- [ ] Every sourced slide has direct URLs in speaker notes.
- [ ] Every slide was rendered and inspected individually.
- [ ] Montage was inspected for rhythm and consistency.
- [ ] Overflow/collision test passes.
- [ ] Final PPTX opens and the intended output filename is exact.
